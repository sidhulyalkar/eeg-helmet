Evidence boundary

This is a concept visualization, not a hardware wiring plan or an approved wearable. Colored dots are placeholders and the ear forms are generic loops. A synthetic fit does not establish skin pressure, biocompatibility, electrical isolation, acoustic quality, real electrode coverage, or any EEG benefit. Never place it under a protective sports helmet.
